Show the customer a set of products as real cards — a picture, the price, and buttons to put one in their basket. Call this EVERY time a product comes into the conversation for the first time, whether it is ONE product or several (from a search, a photo, or a product the customer names), and then write ONE short sentence, which may also answer their question. Never list products in your own text: a numbered list in a chat has no pictures, no prices anybody can tap and no way to buy. Pass the product ids in the order you want them shown, comma-separated, up to ten; the customer sees five at a time with a See more button. It answers shown, total and hasMore. `shown` can be lower than what you asked for when a product has gone off sale since your search — say what was actually shown. Once the customer has already seen a product in this conversation, answer follow-up questions about it in a sentence without drawing it again. Asking the customer to confirm a product is the one they mean always needs its card if they have not seen it.
